# Pearl Labs LLC

Veteran-founded software for defense planning and the work in the field.

[Company](https://pearllab.io/) · [ARGUS](https://argusplatform.ai/) · [FenceEstimatePro](https://fenceestimatepro.com/) · [Company brief](https://pearllab.io/company-brief)

## What we build

**[ARGUS Platform](https://github.com/pearl-labs-llc/argus-platform)** brings defense analysis, combat modeling, wargaming, sustainment and historical research into a shared planning workspace. The aim is to make an estimate's reasoning available for a person to examine and challenge.

**[FenceEstimatePro](https://github.com/pearl-labs-llc/fenceestimatepro)** connects measured fence layouts, supplier prices, customer proposals, job coordination and project financials.

## How we work

- State the assumptions behind an assessment.
- Keep supporting sources connected to numbers and findings.
- Preserve human authority over decisions.

Our [public forecast receipts](https://github.com/pearl-labs-llc/argus-forecast-receipts) document one ARGUS research experiment through commitments, disclosures and scoring rules. The experiment has not yet demonstrated predictive skill; its separate automated confirmer is not a third-party auditor.
